Where Grace Meets Purpose

— Ezekiel 34:15 (KJV)

Our Story

The name ALL STARS ASSEMBLY is not a branding choice — it is a theological conviction drawn from Ezekiel 34:15, where God declares that He Himself will shepherd His people and cause them to lie down in a place of life, safety, and provision.

“I will feed my flock, and I will cause them to lie down, saith the Lord GOD.”
Ezekiel 34:15 — KJV

Founded on 17th September 2000, ALL STARS ASSEMBLY began as a small gathering of believers who took this divine promise seriously. Where earthly shepherds had left people scattered and without rest, God promised to personally lead, feed, heal, and restore — and this ministry exists to be a living expression of that covenant. Over two decades on, we have grown into a vibrant, Spirit-filled church family committed to the Great Commission, with branches spreading across the region and a global reach through media and prayer.

We believe every person is created with God-given purpose, and our mandate is to help every soul discover their identity in Christ and walk in the divine destiny He has prepared for them.

What We Believe

  • The Holy Trinity — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit
  • The authority and inspiration of Scripture
  • Salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ
  • Baptism of the Holy Spirit with evidence of speaking in tongues
  • Divine healing and the resurrection of the dead
  • The second coming of Christ

Our Vision

To be a lighthouse church that illuminates our city, transforms lives, and raises a generation of Spirit-filled believers who impact every sphere of society for God's glory.

Our Mission

To preach the undiluted Gospel of Jesus Christ, make disciples of all nations, and demonstrate God's Kingdom through signs, wonders, and acts of compassion in our community.
