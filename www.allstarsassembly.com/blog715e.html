Revenue, pastor Rev P.S White to Minister at Worship & Word Experience in San Antonio, Texas

Admin Oct 23, 2026 668 views
Revenue, pastor Dr. Rev P.S White is currently in the United States of America for a special ministry engagement at House of Abundance Church in San Antonio, Texas, where he will be ministering at the upcoming Worship & Word Experience.

The Worship & Word Experience is a special gathering focused on the power of worship and the teaching of God’s Word. It promises to be a time of spiritual renewal, fellowship, worship, and an encounter with God.

Revenue, pastor Rev P.S White is scheduled to minister as the Guest Speaker at the service taking place on Sunday, 25th October 2026.

Service Schedule

📖 Sunday School: 9:00 AM
🔥 Celebration Service: 10:00 AM

📍 Venue:
House of Abundance Church
3655 Fredericksburg Rd
San Antonio, TX 78201, USA

The service will also be available through live streaming on @Hoachurchtx, providing an opportunity for those who are unable to attend physically to connect and participate from wherever they are.

A Ministry of the Word

Revenue, pastor Rev P.S White's ministry continues to reach people beyond geographical boundaries, with a passion for teaching God's Word, strengthening believers, and encouraging people to pursue a deeper relationship with Christ.

This ministry engagement in San Antonio is another opportunity to share the Word of God and fellowship with believers in the United States.

We look forward to a powerful Worship & Word Experience and pray that lives will be transformed through worship and the ministry of God's Word.

Join us in prayer as Revenue, pastor Rev P.S White ministers in San Antonio, Texas.

May Christ be glorified, and may His Word continue to reach hearts and transform lives.
