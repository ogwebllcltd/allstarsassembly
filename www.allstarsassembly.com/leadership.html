Biography

Revenue, pastor Dr. Rev P.S White is the founder and Lead Pastor of ALL STARS ASSEMBLY — a vibrant, multi-cultural ministry committed to raising leaders and transforming communities. Born on 1st April 1966 in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria, he gave his life to Christ in 1982 and has remained committed to the service of God ever since.

With a profound anointing and a clear mandate from God, he travels the nations equipping the church to fulfil the Great Commission and walk in dominion. As a passionate preacher, author, and teacher, his life's work is dedicated to soul-winning and fostering deep spiritual growth in believers across the globe.

On 17th September 2000, he co-founded ALL STARS ASSEMBLY Christian Assembly (now ALL STARS ASSEMBLY) alongside his wife, Pastor Mrs. Favour Akhere, in Asaba, Delta State. Over 25 years, the ministry has grown into a multi-branch, multi-cultural organisation headquartered in Asaba, with branches in Benin City and beyond. He hosts the television broadcast "Prophetic Flight", publishes the monthly devotional "Daily Life Meadows", and the "Life Meadows Magazine".

He serves as National President of the Pastor's Forum for Sustainable Development (PFSD), reflecting his commitment to leadership development across the body of Christ in Nigeria. He has been honoured with an Integrity Award by Character Media Incorporated and holds an Honorary Doctorate in Leadership from New Dimension Seminary, Lagos.

THE JOURNEY

Ministry Timeline

1966
Born in Benin City

Born on 1st April 1966 in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria, to Mr. and Mrs. Michael Akhere Agbonkhesan of Esan North LGA.

1982
Born Again

Gave his life to Christ as a young man and committed himself fully to the service of God from that point forward.

1988
Early Career

Relocated to Asaba, Delta State, where he began building his foundation — both in commerce and in spiritual purpose.

1990s
Education & Training

Pursued both academic and biblical education — graduating from Adekunle Ajasin University (B.Sc. Public Administration) and completing training at two Bible colleges in Delta State.

2000
Church Founded

On 17th September 2000, co-founded ALL STARS ASSEMBLY Christian Assembly alongside his wife, Pastor Mrs. Favour Akhere, in Asaba, Delta State.

2000s
"Prophetic Flight" Launched

Launched the television broadcast "Prophetic Flight" to reach souls beyond the four walls of the church, alongside the Life Meadows Magazine and Daily Devotional.

2010s
Expansion

The ministry expanded to multiple branches including Benin City and has grown into a multi-cultural congregation hosting thousands across Nigeria.

Today
25+ Years — Still Growing

Over 25 years, Revenue, pastor Dr. Rev P.S White leads the Influence Conference (May), annual Convention (November), and continues to equip leaders across the nations.
