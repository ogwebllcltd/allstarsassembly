About This Event

GROW & GLOW 2026 is a special gathering presented by the Kingdom Eagles of ALL STARS ASSEMBLY , designed to bring together a generation that desires to grow in God, seek His presence, and shine His light in the world.

Under the theme “The Generation That Seek God” , inspired by Psalm 24:6, this special event will be a powerful time of worship, music, fellowship, and the ministry of God's Word.

Hosted by Revenue, pastor Rev P.S White , GROW & GLOW 2026 will bring together worshippers, young people, ministers, and lovers of God for an unforgettable experience centered on faith, spiritual growth, and a deeper pursuit of God.

Come with an expectant heart and be part of a generation that chooses to seek God wholeheartedly.

Come and GROW. Come and GLOW.
